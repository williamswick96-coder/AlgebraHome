Class Schedule

In-class instruction takes place twice a week every Tuesday and Thursday. Attendance and active participation during lectures are required.

Homework Routine

Short daily assignments (~30 minutes) are assigned for Monday, Wednesday, Friday, and Saturday out of Holt Algebra 2.

Assessments

  • Checkpoint Challenge Assignments: Completed at the end of each chapter.
  • Fall Semester: 2 In-Class Tests.
  • Spring Semester: 3 In-Class Tests + Comprehensive Final Exam.

Accessing Your Work & Grade Tokens

To submit homework grade tokens: Copy and paste the grade token available after completing all problems on that day. Dropdown sub-pages in the upper right contain modules for each non-school day. Daily work is strongly recommended.
