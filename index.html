Accessing Your Work

To submit homework grade tokens: Copy and paste the grade token available after completing all problems for that day. Use the dropdown in the upper right to jump to sections. Daily 30-minute work is strongly recommended.

Class Schedule

In-class instruction takes place twice a week every Tuesday and Thursday. Attendance and active participation are required.

Homework Routine

Short daily assignments (~30 to 40 minutes) are assigned for Wednesday, Friday, Saturday, and Monday out of Holt Algebra 2.

Assessments

  • Checkpoint Challenge Assignments: At chapter end.
  • Fall Semester: 2 In-Class Tests.
  • Spring Semester: 3 In-Class Tests + 2-Part Final Exam.
