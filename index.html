Course Overview

Welcome to Algebra 2 at Saint Peter the Rock Academy. This portal outlines our complete academic year schedule covering Chapters 1 through 14. Pacing is carefully balanced with one week dedicated to review chapters and two weeks allocated to core chapters. Daily problem sets reinforce key concepts introduced during in-class instruction.

Assessment & Routine

In-class instruction occurs every Tuesday and Thursday. Students complete focused homework problem sets on Mondays, Wednesdays, Fridays, and Saturdays. Chapter Checkpoint assignments are completed on Mondays following each chapter's completion.
