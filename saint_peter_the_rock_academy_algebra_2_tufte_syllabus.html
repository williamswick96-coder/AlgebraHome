I.Course Structure & Homework Protocol

Welcome to Algebra 2 at Saint Peter the Rock Academy. The curriculum spans Chapters 1 through 14 of Holt Algebra 2 with tailored pacing: one week each for foundational review chapters (Chapters 1, 2, and 4; Chapter 3 spans two weeks across Thanksgiving), and two weeks each for core Algebra 2 chapters (Chapters 5 through 14).

Weekly Rhythm & Instruction

In-class instruction convenes twice weekly every Tuesday and Thursday. Students are expected to bring their textbook, maintain an organized derivation notebook, and participate actively during worked demonstrations. Between class meetings, short daily problem sets (~30 minutes) are scheduled for Monday, Wednesday, Friday, and Saturday.

Assessments & Grade Verification Tokens

Mastery is evaluated through chapter-end Checkpoint Challenge Assignments, two in-class examinations during the Fall Semester, and three in-class examinations plus a cumulative two-part Final Exam during the Spring Semester. For digital practice modules, complete all assigned problems for the day, copy the generated MATH-GRADE::... verification token, and paste it directly into your JupiterEd assignment submission box.

1 Daily 30-minute discipline. Consistent four-day-a-week practice (M, W, F, Sa) prevents cognitive overload before Tuesday and Thursday seminars. Never batch all four homework sets into a single evening.
